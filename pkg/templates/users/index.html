ELEVATING FINANCIAL JOURNALISM. EMPOWERING PUBLIC UNDERSTANDING.

x Moniepoint X Lagos Business School Financial Literacy Programme for Journalists

The Moniepoint–Lagos Business School Financial Literacy Programme for Journalists is a pioneering initiative created to empower Nigerian journalists with the expertise to accurately and insightfully report on financial services and the fast-evolving fintech space.

Apply now →
X

This programme is a result of a strategic collaboration between Moniepoint, one of Nigeria’s most influential financial technology companies, and Lagos Business School (LBS)—a premier business school in Africa with a strong reputation for delivering impactful executive education to industry leaders, policymakers, and professionals.

In today’s complex and rapidly changing financial environment, journalists are expected to do more than report—they must interpret and contextualise financial developments, making them accessible and meaningful to the public. This programme is built to support that responsibility.
